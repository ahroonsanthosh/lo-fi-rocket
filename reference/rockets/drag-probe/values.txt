# Part 2: OpenRocket's flight data use the same drag coefficient. In reference/rockets/timeseries (wind 0), the
# drag coefficient at Mach 0.10, 0.14 and 0.18 is the same thrusting and coasting and equals Part 1's static
# value to 0.0004 (R1: 0.77334 against 0.77335 at Mach 0.1; R3 0.87798 / 0.87705 against 0.87769), and the drag
# force is 0.5 rho V^2 A_ref C_D with ISA density within 0.04 % at every sample checked, thrusting and coasting.
# With pods (reference/rockets/pod-probe/motors.txt) the drag coefficient is also the same with the motors in
# the core, in the pods or both.
#
# Part 3 (ProtuberanceProbe): rail button drag. Files beside this one:
# - protuberance-sweeps.csv: one variable at a time from a 10 x 10 mm button (inner 6 mm, base and flange 2 mm),
#   two buttons 0.1 m apart, front 0.35 m behind a 0.1 m ogive nose on a 25 mm tube, Mach 0.3; and launch lugs.
#   Columns: x is from the body tube's front (add noseLength for the nose tip); deltaCd is the drag coefficient
#   with the parts minus without, on refArea.
# - rail-button-collapse.csv: the same eta = H / (0.37 x Re_x^-0.2) at eight positions, Mach 0.3 and 1.5:
#   the coefficient agrees within 0.05 %.
# - rail-button-table.csv: single buttons (outer 12, inner 6 mm, no base or flange) over Mach 0.01 to 5 and eta
#   0.1 to 100 (tag "table"), and 80 random buttons kept out of the table (tag "check").
# - rail-button-temperature.csv: one button at 263.15, 293.15 and 313.15 K, with OpenRocket's viscosity.
# Findings: per button C_D A = K (OD (base + flange) + ID (height - base - flange)), exactly linear in each; K
# depends on Mach and eta only; nothing depends on the rocket's length, the body radius, the fins or the other
# buttons; each button counts at its own position. Below a total height of about 4 mm OpenRocket's figure stops
# falling (its minimum height), so the table uses heights of 6 mm or more.
# Launch lugs: OpenRocket's lug drag does not depend on position; it is linear in the lug's length (a term
# proportional to length plus a constant), so it is not the stagnation-pressure lug of Niskanen eq. (3.95):
# R1's lug is 0.00755 in OpenRocket against 0.01227 by eq. (3.95) at Mach 0.1.
#
