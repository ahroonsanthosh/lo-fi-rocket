# Plan 0007: Motor thrust and mass model, and the RockSim `.rse` reader

**Status:** written 2026-10-12 under autonomous mode; implemented in the following commits.
**Phase:** 2, step 1 (CLAUDE.md 6.6). The ThrustCurve.org search and cache, CSV import and the user-entered curve are Phase 4 (interoperability); the architecture here must carry them without a rewrite (CLAUDE.md "Primary user").

## Goal

A format-independent motor definition in `packages/physics` that gives thrust, impulse delivered, propellant remaining, motor mass and motor CG at any time, from either a RASP `.eng` motor (plan 0004) or a RockSim `.rse` motor (this plan), or from an explicit mass profile (liquid engines later). Every quantity is interpolated or integrated from the file's data by stated rules, and the rule used is recorded in the result.

## Sources

- CLAUDE.md 6.6: "Interpolate thrust linearly between data points. Mass during burn must follow the source format's rules; document which."
- ThrustCurve.org, _RASP File Format_ (`reference/docs/thrustcurve-api/raspformat.html`): data points are (time s, thrust N), implicit origin, final thrust zero marks the burn time; the format says nothing about mass during the burn.
- Apogee Components, _RockSim & EngEdit Engine File (.rse) Format Guide_ (`reference/docs/apogee-rocksim-engine-file-format.pdf`, p. 1): attributes and their meaning; `<data>` points `t`, `f` required, `m`, `cg` optional; with `auto-calc-mass="1"` RockSim uses m(t) = initMass − (propMass/burnTime)·t and with `auto-calc-cg="1"` the CG is fixed at the engine centre; units length mm, mass "g or kg", thrust N, time s; delays comma-separated.
- ThrustCurve.org, _Motor Statistics_ and _Glossary_ (`reference/docs/thrustcurve-api/motorstats.html`, `glossary.html`): total impulse = thrust × time over the burn (N·s); specific impulse = total impulse over the weight of the propellant, in seconds.
- SI Brochure 9th ed., Appendix 1, 3rd CGPM 1901 declaration 3, printed p. 154: standard acceleration due to gravity g_n = 9.80665 m/s² (for the weight in the specific impulse definition only).
- Niskanen (2009) p. 14: "The thrust of a motor is directly proportional to the velocity of the escaping gas and the mass per time unit that is exhausted." With a constant exhaust velocity this makes the propellant consumed proportional to the impulse delivered, which is the RASP mass rule adopted below (D-028).
- Observed, not a source: the seven ThrustCurve `.rse` files of the reference motors carry an `m` column equal to propWt·(1 − I(t)/I_total) to 0.02 g (verified in the plan 0007 scan and asserted in the tests) and a constant `cg` within 0.5 mm of half the length; OpenRocket's reference values were generated with the same impulse-proportional rule and mid-length CG (`reference/rockets/settings.md`).

## Decisions

- D-028: the RASP mass rule is impulse-proportional with the CG fixed at mid-length. The alternative, RockSim's linear-in-time auto-calculation, is a RockSim convention for files without an `m` column and is used only there.
- D-029: `.rse` masses are in grams or kilograms per the guide; the reader decides by the specific-impulse check when `Isp` is present and exactly one unit is consistent within 2 %, and otherwise requires the `massUnit` option. The `m` column is interpreted as propellant remaining when it runs from propWt to 0, as total mass when it runs from initWt to initWt − propWt, and rejected otherwise. A `cg` column is accepted only when constant and within 1 mm of half the length (the guide's "engine centre"; the reference point of a varying `cg` is not documented), and then the CG is taken as exactly half the length; a varying `cg` throws `NotImplementedError` until a documented hybrid-motor file is available.
- Thrust before the first explicit point: RASP prepends the origin; an `.rse` file must start at t = 0 (all seven do); a first point at t > 0 is rejected rather than extrapolated.
- A motor's mass never increases: validated on explicit profiles, inherent in MOT-3.

## Tests

- MOT-1: D12 thrust at t = 0.2 s by hand: between (0.184, 17.275) and (0.237, 24.258): 17.275 + 6.983 × 0.016/0.053 = 19.383 N; at t = 0 → 0; at t = 1.65 → 0; at 2 s → 0.
- MOT-2: D12 impulse to 0.282 s by hand (five trapezoids: 0.0629 + 0.3999 + 0.9059 + 1.1006 + 1.2147 = 3.684 N·s); total 16.839 N·s (the `.rse` file's `Itot`); partial trapezoid at 0.2 s.
- MOT-3: D12 propellant remaining at 0.282 s = 0.0211 × (1 − 3.684/16.839) = 0.016484 kg (the `.rse` `m` column prints 16.4837 g); mass at liftoff 0.0426, at burnout 0.0215; CG 0.035 m.
- MOT-4: G80T from `.rse` with its `m` column equals the RASP rule within 0.02 g at every data point; the guide's auto-calculation on a file without `m` gives m(t) linear in t (hand value at mid-burn).
- MOT-5: the seven files are detected as grams; the guide's own example (`initWt="0.063"`, kg) is detected as kilograms when an `Isp` consistent with kg is added, and rejected without `Isp` unless the unit is given.
- `.rse` reader: every attribute of the seven files read; `dia`, `len`, masses, delays and burn time agree with the RASP headers (lengths within 1 mm, D-029 notes the H128W/H238T 1 mm discrepancies); malformed inputs (missing attribute, non-numeric, decreasing time, negative thrust, `m` not matching either interpretation, varying `cg`, two engines, empty data).
- Properties (fast-check): thrust ≥ 0; impulse non-decreasing; propellant remaining non-increasing, within [0, m_prop]; motor mass in [casing, total]; identical inputs give identical outputs; the rule used is named in every result.
