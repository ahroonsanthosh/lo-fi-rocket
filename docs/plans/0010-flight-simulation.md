# Plan 0010: Flight simulation (6DOF, integrators, events, recovery, outputs)

**Status:** written 2026-10-12 under autonomous mode; implemented in three commits (A aerodynamic forces and moments, B dynamics and integration, C verification against analytical cases, convergence and OpenRocket).
**Phase:** 2, step 4 (CLAUDE.md 6.5), the last Phase 2 step.

## Goal

Simulate a flight from ignition on the rail to landing: six degrees of freedom with the quaternion orientation of plan 0008, forces from thrust (plan 0007), gravity (plan 0008), the normal force and axial drag of chapters 3.2 and 3.4 at the current angle of attack, pitch and roll damping; events located by root finding (rail exit, burnout, ejection charge, apogee, deployment, altitude crossings, landing); a three-degree-of-freedom descent under the recovery devices; RK4 with a fixed step and an adaptive Dormand–Prince RK5(4) integrator; a time series of every state variable and the summary values of CLAUDE.md 6.5. A three-degree-of-freedom point-mass mode (velocity-aligned, zero angle of attack) exists only as a cross-check and is labelled as such.

## Conventions

- State: position and velocity in the world frame (m, m/s); orientation quaternion body→world; angular velocity in the world frame (rad/s); time since ignition. Body-frame angular velocity for Euler's equation is q⁻¹ ω q.
- Mass, CG and inertia at time t from `rocketMassProperties` with `motorMassAt(t)`; the inertia is about the current CG.
- Force application points: thrust along the nose direction through the CG (no moment, Niskanen p. 14); gravity at the CG; normal force at the CP; axial drag along the axis through the CG; damping moments as pure couples.
- Modes carried in every result: aerodynamic mode (Niskanen displayed, or OpenRocket-equivalent: no fin roll reduction and the base drag rule 'always'); gravity model; integrator; whether the cross-check 3DOF mode was used.
- Simulation ends at touchdown or at the maximum time, with the reason recorded.

## Tests

- Aerodynamics: at α = 0 the normal force is zero and the CP equals plan 0006's; at α = 5° the fin term is linear, the nose term carries sin α/α (0.99873 at 5°), the body lift of R1's tube K A_plan sin²α/A_ref = 1.1 × (0.05 × 0.5) × 0.0075961/1.9635e-3 = 0.10641 at its centre (x = 0.35); the normal force direction opposes the lateral airflow and the moment restores a stable rocket (sign test); pitch damping by hand for a 0.6 m body at 10 rad/s and 50 m/s; roll damping sum for R1's fins by hand ((0.15 × 0.025² × 0.08)/2 + (0.2 × 0.025 × 0.0064)/3 + (0.25 × 0.000512)/12 = 3.75e-6 + 1.0667e-5 + 1.0667e-5 = 2.5083e-5 m⁴).
- Integrators: ẏ = −y and the harmonic oscillator against the exact solutions; RK4 error falls 16× per halving (order 4); the Dormand–Prince fifth-order solution's error falls 32× per halving and its embedded fourth-order estimate tracks the true error (both check the tableau); adaptive stepping meets a 10⁻⁹ tolerance on the oscillator over 100 periods.
- Dynamics: with no aerodynamics and constant gravity the vacuum apogee is v²/2g (CLAUDE.md 7.2); constant thrust with constant mass and no drag matches the kinematic solution; a vertical rail with zero wind keeps α = 0 to 10⁻⁹; the rail exit is located at the rail length to 10⁻⁹ m by root finding; energy is conserved in drag-free coast within 10⁻⁹ relative (fast-check over initial speeds).
- Events: apogee found where the vertical velocity crosses zero; deployment at apogee with and without delay; altitude deployment on descent only; landing at z = 0; burnout at the motor's burn time exactly.
- Convergence: halving the RK4 step from 0.01 s to 0.005 s changes R1's apogee by less than the documented tolerance; RK4 at 0.001 s and RK45 at tolerance 10⁻⁸ agree within the documented tolerance.
- OpenRocket acceptance (R1 to R4, zero wind and 5 m/s, OpenRocket-equivalent modes): apogee within 5 %, maximum velocity within 3 %; rail-exit velocity, time to apogee, deployment velocity and ground-hit velocity reported with their differences. R5 reported as not simulable past Mach 0.8 (D-034).
- Determinism: two runs give identical time series; NaN injected into a derivative stops the run with a `NonFiniteResultError` naming the variable and time.
